## Reading catalog

The reading catalog in `src/lib/stoic/catalog.ts` contains public-domain Stoic
texts and translations. Each book carries a `sourceUrl` pointing at the source
used for that text (usually Wikisource or another public-domain archive). The
catalog is data, not a claim of exclusive rights.

If you contribute a new translation or excerpt:

- Make sure the text is in the public domain, or that you have permission to
  redistribute it.
- Add the canonical source URL to the book entry.
- Do not copy a modern copyrighted translation just because it appears on a
  website.

## Curated quotes

`src/lib/stoic/quotes-data.ts` contains short quotations and attributions. Keep
attributions accurate. When a quotation's attribution is disputed, note that in
the entry rather than presenting it as certain.
